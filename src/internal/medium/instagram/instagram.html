Instagram

How to obtain the session key
  1. Log in at instagram.com in your browser.
  2. Open DevTools (F12) → Application (Chrome) / Storage (Firefox) → Cookies → https://www.instagram.com.
  3. Copy the value of the sessionid cookie.

The key is not stored — paste it every time you sync. It becomes invalid when you log out of Instagram or change your password.

⚠️ Warning: the Instagram session ID grants full account access for a few hours to whoever has it. Consider logging out of your Instagram account after syncing the footprint to revoke the session ID.
